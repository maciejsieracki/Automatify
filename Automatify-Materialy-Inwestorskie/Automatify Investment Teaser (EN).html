A hub for AI agents and humanoid robots built for mid-sized companies

Automatify replaces the expensive man-hour with a robot-hour at a fraction of the cost — one partner, one contract, both worlds of automation. Goal: the leading automation hub in Poland and CEE, IPO in 2031.

replace workers with agents. agentify it. replace workers with robots. robotify it.
Problem
SMEs have no way to adopt AI

Mid-sized companies lack both the team and the budget to implement AI on their own. Low automation levels put them at risk of losing competitiveness against firms already cutting labour costs. The market is fragmented — clients must stitch together multiple vendors.

A shrinking physical workforce

Poland will lose 4 million working-age people by 2040. Robotics is the answer, yet 97% of SMEs operate without a single physical robot — blocked by CAPEX, missing skills and the absence of an integrator serving smaller companies.

Solution — two pillars, one platform
AgentifyAgents-as-a-Service

Agentify digital workers: AI agents and RPA bots (software licences) taking over repetitive back-office processes 24/7.

  • Pay-as-you-Save — no up-front fee: the PLN 60k per-process implementation is repaid through the subscription over 4 years; subscription equals 10% of the savings generated
  • Marketplace of ready-made packages: 24/7 customer service, invoices→ERP, reporting, reconciliation
  • Robot-hour billing · Client Portal with live savings measurement
  • EBITDA break-even 2029 · EBITDA margin 2035 ~70%
RobotifyRobots-as-a-Service

Robotify physical robots: humanoids, cobots and AMRs — the client buys the robot, we bill its work like an employee.

  • Humanoids (Figure / 1X / Apptronik) for tasks that need human form: hospitality, healthcare, reception
  • Cobots and AMRs for manufacturing, packing and logistics
  • Robot CAPEX on the client’s side (EU / BGK grant or own budget) · software, integration and service on subscription · 99% SLA◆ · 24-hour hot-swap◆ · 36-month contracts◆
  • EBITDA break-even 2029 · EBITDA margin 2035 ~77%

Competitive advantage
All-in-one

One partner instead of many vendors — from a software agent to a humanoid robot, under a single contract and a single billing model.

Risk-free model

The client pays nothing up front for an Agentify deployment — it is repaid out of the savings generated. The Robotify physical robot is bought by the client, while we deliver the software, integration and service on subscription. Incentives are fully aligned.

Local + compliant

The Polish and CEE mid-market that global vendors do not serve. EU AI Act compliance as a barrier to entry.
