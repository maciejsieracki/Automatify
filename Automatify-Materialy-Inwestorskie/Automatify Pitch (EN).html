02 · Problem

Mid-sized firms are losing the efficiency battle

The problems run in two directions: no digitization of office processes (a job for agents) and no hands for physical work (a job for robots). Classic vendors target the Fortune 500 — the SME segment is dramatically underserved.

01.

No awareness or process map

SMEs don't know what can be automated or which processes are candidates. No mapped-out processes, no in-house AI team. Zero entry point.

02.

High deployment costs

Classic AI/RPA deployments: €24k–118k upfront. Physical robots: $35k–150k CAPEX. A mid-sized company has neither the budget nor the appetite for risk without guaranteed ROI.

03.

A fragmented vendor market

The client assembles RPA, OCR, chatbot, integrator, robot vendor, safety, and service alone. No single partner is accountable for the business outcome — only for one module.

04.

No hands for physical work

Demographics + labor emigration. SMEs can't find warehouse, production, or hospitality staff. A physical robot is the only scalable labor resource from 2026 on.

The cost of inaction. One office FTE ≈ €55k/yr · one warehouse operator ≈ €22k/yr. Every non-automated process is burned budget.

Agentify · 07.2 · Framework

Agentify stack — UiPath + n8n dziś, własny orkiestrator jutro

Our framework binds LLMs, memory, tools, and context into a coherent agent. Under the hood today: UiPath + n8n. Series A plan (MVP3): our own orchestrator built on LangGraph — full control of the execution layer, our own IP, vendor independence.

What does the Agentify framework add?

The framework is the code layer that lets an LLM act on its own: plan steps, use tools (API, database, calculator), remember context, and make decisions. An LLM alone only generates text — the framework turns it into an agent that does the work.

M

Multi-tenant

Each client in an isolated context. Full GDPR compliance, EU-only data residency.

P

Memory layer

A vector store on client data (pgvector). Persistent context memory across sessions.

T

Tool registry

Standardized connectors to the client's ERP / CRM / API. Plug-and-play for the integrator.

R

Per-task routing

Model choice by context: GPT-4o, Claude, Mistral. Cost/quality optimization.

O

Observability

An audit of every decision + cost tracking + SLA. The client sees what, when, and at what cost.

UiPath + n8n — FOUNDATION

Two pillars of the execution layer in MVP1/MVP2. UiPath — a proven enterprise layer for RPA processes, AI Computer Vision, integration with legacy systems without an API, and SLA monitoring. n8n — an open-source, self-hosted AI-workflow orchestrator with 400+ API integrations. Together they cover the full spectrum: from enterprise-grade RPA to lightweight AI agents. Series A (MVP3): migration to our own orchestrator built on LangGraph.

state graphs: each node = an agent step (e.g. "check CRM," "send email"), each edge = a transition condition. Production-ready: cycles, checkpointing, human-in-the-loop.

Robotify · 08.2 · Humanoid technologies

The humanoid stack — a general-purpose form, human tasks

The stack for humanoid robots: scene perception, VLM reasoning, motion planning, safety in human environments.

01.
Vision-Language Models
High-level reasoning

VLMs as the decision layer (GPT-4V, Claude, OpenVLA, RT-2). Translating natural-language commands into action sequences. Self-correction: the robot sees its errors and adjusts the plan.

02.
Whole-body Control
Locomotion + balance

Reinforcement learning + MPC for bipedal gait. Stability on stairs, uneven surfaces, and around people. 8 hours of work per charge.

03.
Imitation Learning
Learning by demonstration

The robot learns new tasks from 5–20 human demonstrations (teleoperation or VR). Used by Figure, 1X, and Apptronik to scale without programming.

04.
Human-Robot Interaction
Safety + UX

ISO 13482 (assistive robots). Force-limited operation: collaboration without fencing. Speech, gestures, eye-tracking — human-like interaction for hospitality and care.

Humanoids — where the task needs a human form: hospitality, healthcare, reception, senior assistance.

In 2027–2028 no bank will be available, so the fund finances both streams. The deployment portfolio does not yet have the repayment history a bank requires, which is why the facility columns read zero in both scenarios — the working-capital need of PLN 1,358,825 at end-2027 and PLN 31,042,444 at end-2028 is covered by the fund alongside growth capital. Caveats to the capital ask — to be read together with the figures.
  • 78.7% of the peak is accumulated tax. CIT paid in cash cumulatively to Aug 2030 is PLN 124,631,524 against a growth-capital peak of PLN 158,283,690. Without tax the peak in that same month would be PLN 33,652,166. The capital ask is in large part a request to fund tax paid before the company has accumulated the cash.
  • The tax payment convention is an assumption. CIT for year Y is paid in 12 instalments of 1/12 with a one-month lag — in line with Polish monthly-advance practice. It is the more conservative of the two conventions considered; a "whole amount in March of the following year" convention would produce a markedly lower ask.
  • The peak is a plateau, not a spike. Three months sit within 10% of the maximum: Jul–Sep 2030. September 2030 gives PLN 190,973,288 against PLN 193,044,228 in August — a difference of 1.1%. That is why we quote the whole July–September window rather than August alone.
Three caveats to the projections — they stand next to the figures, not in a footnote.
  • Robotify pricing rule. We do not sell servicing below twice the cost of serving a given country. This is a deliberate commercial policy: we would rather not sign a contract than service it below the point where it pays. In 2029 the market is still early and this rule sets most of Robotify revenue; as the fleet builds out in later years the market sets the price, not the rule.
  • Tax assumption. The projections assume a 19% rate and the deduction of research spending from the tax base; confirmation from a tax adviser is in progress.
  • An owned robot fleet. The projections contain no return from this option — it is upside beyond the figures shown.
